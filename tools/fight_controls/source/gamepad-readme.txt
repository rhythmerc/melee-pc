Hiya there!

This is collection of current gen gamepad vector I made, and I made the back
trigger button visible. I made those vector gamepad out of my need for having
gamepad settings in my own game, Past Future, and what I found on the web
doesn't have back trigger buttons visible. That's what drives my incentive to
make those assets.

This collection includes SVG file you can modify in your vector designer app
of choice, and also respective PNG renders in 2048x2048. I applied CC0 license
to those assets.

Feel free to use this in your project, either with or without crediting me.
Although it would be nice if you give me little shoutout in the OpenGameArt
comment section and show me what you're using the asset on. :D

Happy developing!
